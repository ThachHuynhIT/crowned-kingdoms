Create one pixel-art CARD ILLUSTRATION for Crowned Kingdoms: "Hợp Đồng Đánh Thuê" — Hợp Đồng Đánh Thuê | Trả toàn bộ vàng (tối đa 16); trận năm nay có thêm 1 lính đánh thuê tại Ngai cho mỗi 2 vàng đã trả.
What it is: a spell: a spellbook, scroll or one iconic spell effect (not a building and not a character)
Format: LANDSCAPE aspect ratio 4:3 (target 1024x768 px, to be reduced to about 96x72 art pixels, so each art pixel is about 10x10 source pixels). One clear subject, centred, filling about 70% of the frame, with a simple themed scene behind it. Buildings and units follow the game's view: Stardew-style 2.5D looking down at 45 degrees, front facing, roof or top visible (see the approved reference); spells and enchantments are one iconic magical object with its effect.
Colour: ONE dominant hue (sea teal) with 2-3 tints and shades and one small warm accent; strong silhouette, large simple shapes, no tiny details: it must still read at 48x36 px.
Style: warm cozy medieval fantasy pixel art, crisp square pixel clusters, upper-left lighting, dark material-colored outlines (never pure black), saturated warm palette, readable material texture.
Background: a stormy teal sea with waves, a ship silhouette and a lighthouse, painted lower-contrast and softer than the subject; no frame, no border, no vignette.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, UI, card frame, watermark, blur, antialiasing or smooth gradients. No characters except those that belong to the subject (soldiers of a troop building).
Kingdom theme: ships, rope, shells, sea.
the SUBJECT is a mercenary contract: a rolled parchment tied with a teal ribbon and a wax seal lying on a pile of gold coins with a cutlass beside it; one iconic object, no building, no people.
